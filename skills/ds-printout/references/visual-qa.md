# Visual QA

A successful render is not visual QA. Look at the page.

## What to inspect

Inspect whole pages and readable crops, covering dense labels, rural coverage,
junctions, the title block, and legend and schedule edges.

Check clipping, overlaps, missing layers, legend categories, units, table scope,
visible furniture alignment, adjacent A0 schedules, and the requested
top-centered name — all before batching.

Ground scale and quantities in owner output, never in pixel measurements.

Revise, rerender and inspect again.

## Coverage, and what may be claimed

Review every page of a small set. For a batch, review templates and outliers and
record the exact coverage reviewed.

Sampling never becomes an every-page claim. State what was actually inspected.

See also [sheet furniture](sheet-furniture.md) for the placement rules the
inspection is checking against, and [acceptance](acceptance.md) before release.
