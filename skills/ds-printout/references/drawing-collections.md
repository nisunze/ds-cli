# Combined drawing delivery

Discover the governed Combined Report command for sector or project delivery.
Verify its multipage A0 and A3 collections against the member inventory: page
count, source order, paper sizes and complete-project sheet numbering.

A local bundle can also join digest-pinned PDFs through the reporter's closed
task. Keep each paper/layout in its own collection. Missing or invalid source
PDFs must refuse rather than silently remove drawings. Local packaging alone
is not shared publication; inspect the shared archive registry and download.
